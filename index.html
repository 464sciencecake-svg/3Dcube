3Dcube

Explore • Build • Create

About the Game

3Dcube is a 3D exploration and building game. Explore the world, place blocks, break structures, and create your own builds.

How to Play

Computer: Use WASD to move and Space to jump.

Mobile: Use the joystick to move and the jump button to jump.

Use the button in the top-left corner to enter fullscreen mode.
